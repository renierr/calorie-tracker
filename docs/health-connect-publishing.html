Meals, not duplicates.

NutriScan sends logged meals to the device-local Health Connect store. Cloud-synced meals remain one Health Connect record, even when published from more than one device.

Cross-device identity

clientRecordId = nutriscan:meal:<shortId>

clientRecordVersion = updatedAt

The meal shortId moves with NutriScan cloud sync. Health Connect combines the writing package and client record ID, so publishing the same synced meal on a second device updates its existing record. The newest updatedAt version wins.

MEAL-ABC123XYZ becomes nutriscan:meal:MEAL-ABC123XYZ. An edit raises the version and replaces the nutrition values.

Exact Health Connect mapping

Each NutriScan food entry creates one Android Health Connect NutritionRecord. NutriScan does not create separate calorie or macro records.

Automatic

When enabled, newly saved and edited meals publish immediately. The first request asks only for Nutrition write permission.

Manual

Publish now backfills history and shows live progress. Reconcile deletes NutriScan nutrition records, then recreates the current meal history after a deletion or restore.

Removal

Remove NutriScan data deletes only Nutrition records written by NutriScan. Local meals and data from other apps stay intact.

Scope

  • Published: meal name, calories, protein, carbohydrates, total fat.
  • Not published in v1: activities, calories burned, body weight, photos, notes, and AI confidence.
  • Available only on Android. Windows and Linux do not display the Health Connect settings entry.
